About Dr. Rostova

Dr. Elena Rostova is a fellowship-trained neurologist dedicated to restorative, human-centered diagnostic care. She specializes in the management of complex, refractory migraine syndromes, peripheral neuropathies, and cognitive neurological transitions. Her clinical methodology blends precision neuro-imaging with holistic lifestyle therapies to reduce pain frequency and restore patient quality of life.

Prior to joining HealSync Neurosciences, Dr. Rostova completed advanced clinical research at Harvard Medical School and served on national committees for headache diagnostics. She prioritizes active listening, ensuring that each patient feels fully heard and equipped with an actionable, transparent treatment roadmap.
